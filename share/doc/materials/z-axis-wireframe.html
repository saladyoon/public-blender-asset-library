Z Axis Wireframe Material

The Z Axis Wireframe is a simple material in blue color. It's supposed to be used for preview images and other primitives.

Its colors are based on Gruvbox color scheme.

Outputs

Cycles and EEVEE BSDF

Streamlined material output that favours speed over quality.

Fallback Principled BSDF

For different render engines, GLTF export and textured preview.
